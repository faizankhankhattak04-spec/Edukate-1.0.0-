About SpeakO

English learning built around the student

SpeakO provides personalized 1-to-1 online English classes for Chinese students. Instead of putting every learner into the same lesson, we focus on the student's English level, goals and preferred learning pace.

Our classes are designed to help students build practical English skills across speaking, listening, reading, writing, pronunciation, vocabulary and conversation. We also offer focused learning for kids, beginners, advanced learners, IELTS, travel and academic English.

Classes are delivered online through WeChat, making it simple for students to communicate with their teacher and arrange flexible lessons.
